# EduGenie design brief

## Direction

**Study Signal** — a calm, editorial learning workspace that makes AI feel like a thoughtful tutor rather than a chatbot.

## Design system

- **Movement:** quiet confidence with warm paper surfaces, midnight ink, and electric cyan accents.
- **Core principles:** one clear action per screen, short readable chunks, visible progress, generous whitespace, and accessible contrast.
- **Color philosophy:** a warm ivory canvas (#f7f3ea) keeps long-form study comfortable; ink navy (#182332) anchors navigation; cyan (#16b8c4) signals intelligence and action; amber (#f3b562) highlights learning energy.
- **Layout paradigm:** editorial split-screen hero, then a compact task rail and a wide answer canvas. On mobile, the rail becomes a horizontal scroll row.
- **Signature elements:** a small open-book mark, numbered task cards, soft ruled dividers, pill-shaped status labels, and a “learning signal” eyebrow.
- **Interaction philosophy:** immediate feedback, clear loading state, no mystery buttons, keyboard-friendly controls, and quiz choices that explain wrong answers.
- **Animation:** subtle lift and opacity transitions only; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- **Typography:** system sans stack for UI with strong display scale, tight headings, and comfortable 1.65 line-height for AI output.
- **Brand essence:** capable, kind, focused.
- **Brand voice:** concise, encouraging, plain-spoken.
- **Wordmark/logo:** EDUGENIE wordmark paired with an open-book and spark symbol.
- **Signature brand color:** electric cyan.

## Asset decision

This is a functional learning workspace, not a marketing or editorial site, so no hero photography is needed. A simple project-specific SVG mark is used for the header and favicon.
